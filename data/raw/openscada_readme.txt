OpenWindSCADA - Open Source Wind Turbine SCADA Datasets

Repository Description:
This is a repository of openly available wind turbine SCADA datasets with high-level descriptions, reusable data loaders for convenient CSV import, and a platform for documenting insights related to data quality and malfunctions.

Available Datasets:
1. Kelmarsh Farm (UK, onshore) - 6 turbines, ~99 variables, 5 years data, 10-minute resolution
2. Penmanshiel Farm (UK, onshore) - 14 turbines, >150 variables, 5 years data, 10-minute resolution
3. EDP Open Data (Spain, onshore) - 5 turbines, ~80 variables, 2 years data, 10-minute resolution (currently unavailable)
4. Fuhrländer Farm (5 turbines, 312 variables, 3 years data, 5-minute resolution, Eclipse Public License v2.0)
5. Kaggle Wind Turbine (Turkey, onshore) - 1 turbine, 4 variables, 1 year data, 10-minute resolution
6. Hill of Towie Wind Farm (Scotland, onshore) - 21 turbines, 655 variables, 8.7 years data, 10-minute resolution, CC-BY-4.0

Data Types:
- SCADA signals: wind speed, power output, rotor speed, pitch angle, generator temperature, etc.
- Logs: SCADA error logs, operator annotations
- Annotations: data quality issues, malfunction observations

Key Variables:
- Wind Speed (m/s)
- Active Power (kW)
- Rotor Speed (rpm)
- Pitch Angle (degrees)
- Generator Temperature (°C)
- Ambient Temperature (°C)
- Nacelle Position (degrees)
- Wind Direction (degrees)

Common Faults:
- Gearbox failures
- Generator overheating
- Pitch system malfunctions
- Yaw system issues
- Blade damage
- Electrical system faults
